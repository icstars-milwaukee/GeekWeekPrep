Geek Week Task Card

A pocket card for the 15 tasks — fill a circle per attempt, date every pass, and a facilitator initials it. Print this page (Ctrl+P, landscape letter — two cards per sheet), cut along the dashed line, and hand one to every intern Monday morning. The pace lines mark the three-tasks-a-day rhythm that lands all 15 by Friday.

How it works

Fill one ○ per attempt
Pass — date it, staff initials it
Miss — buffer, sign up again
3 misses — next task; revisit after 15

The rules

Tasks go in order, 1 to 15
On the clock: keyboard only
Silence while someone tests
No task-talk, ever — concepts yes, Tasks 1–15 no
Cheer every pass — loudly
Staff initials every pass

The finish line: all 15 by Friday is the challenge — and it starts with your name on the whiteboard. Sign up early and often. Real geeks debug, learn, and lead. ★
